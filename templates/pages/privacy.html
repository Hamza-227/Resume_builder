Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2024

Your privacy is our default, not an afterthought. Here's exactly how ResumeATS handles your data — in plain English.

The Short Version

We don't store your resume data. Period. Everything you type into the builder or upload to the checker is processed in server memory and discarded immediately. We have no database of resume content.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Resume content (name, experience, skills, education)
  • Uploaded PDF files
  • Email addresses (unless you contact us voluntarily)
  • Passwords (we have no login system)
  • Payment information (everything is free)

Information We May Collect

Analytics: We may use privacy-respecting aggregated analytics (page view counts) to understand how the site is used. No personally identifiable information.

Cookies: We use a single cookie to remember your dark/light mode preference. No tracking cookies.

Contact form submissions: If you message us, we retain your email to respond. We do not add you to any mailing list without explicit consent.

Third-Party Services

Our site may contain affiliate links. We may earn a commission if you purchase through these links, at no cost to you. We only recommend products we genuinely believe are useful.

Data Security

Since we don't store your resume data, there's nothing to breach. Resume content exists only in server RAM during the milliseconds it takes to generate your PDF.
