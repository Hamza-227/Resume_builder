About Us

Built by Job Seekers, for Job Seekers

We got tired of paying $30/month for a resume builder. So we built a better one — free forever.

ResumeATS is a free AI-powered career platform designed to level the playing field. Every job seeker deserves professional-grade tools to compete in today's automated hiring landscape.

Our Mission

75% of resumes are rejected before a human ever sees them. Applicant Tracking Systems filter out qualified candidates because their resume format doesn't match what the software expects — not because they're unqualified. We built ResumeATS to fix that.

Our tools help you understand exactly how ATS software reads your resume, what it scores you on, and precisely what to change to get past the first filter.

Why Free?

Other resume builders charge $20–50/month for features that should be standard. Career tools should be accessible to everyone — fresh graduates, career changers, laid-off professionals, and anyone navigating a competitive job market. We earn through non-intrusive affiliate partnerships with tools we genuinely recommend.
