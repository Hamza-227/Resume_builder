FAQ

Everything you need to know

What is an ATS resume?

ATS software scans resumes before a human sees them. 99% of Fortune 500 companies use it. An ATS-friendly resume uses standard fonts, no tables or images, and exact keyword matches.

Is this really free forever?

Yes. No login, no credit card, no watermark. Download unlimited PDFs instantly. We earn through ads and affiliate links — you never pay.

Do you store my data?

No. Your data is processed in memory and immediately discarded. Nothing is saved to any database or server.

Which ATS systems does this work with?

Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, iCIMS, and Lever — covering 80%+ of Fortune 500 hiring pipelines.

Why only one page?

Recruiters average 7 seconds per resume. One-page resumes have 2× higher callback rates for under-10-year experience. Our builder auto-scales to guarantee one page.

What font does the PDF use?

Times-Roman only — the gold standard for ATS compatibility. Fancy fonts cause ATS parsing failures.
