Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS resume?

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) software is used by 99% of Fortune 500 companies to scan resumes before a human sees them. An ATS-friendly resume uses standard fonts, no tables or images, and exact keyword matches.

Is this resume builder really free?

Yes. 100% free. No login, no credit card, no watermark. Download unlimited resumes as PDF instantly.

Do you store my data?

No. Your resume data is processed in memory and immediately discarded. Nothing is saved to any database or server.

Which ATS systems is this tested against?

Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, iCIMS, and Lever — the five most common enterprise ATS platforms covering 80%+ of Fortune 500 hiring.

Why only one page?

Recruiters spend an average of 7 seconds on a resume. One-page resumes have 2× higher callback rates for candidates with under 10 years of experience. Our builder auto-scales to guarantee exactly one page.

What font does this use?

Times-Roman — the gold standard for ATS compatibility. Fancy fonts like Calibri or custom fonts can cause ATS parsing failures. We use only standard PDF fonts.
