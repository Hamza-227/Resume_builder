# How ATS Systems Actually Work in 2024

Most job seekers don't know this: **75% of resumes are automatically rejected** before a human ever sees them. The gatekeeper is an Applicant Tracking System — software used by 99% of Fortune 500 companies to filter incoming applications.

If your resume isn't optimized for ATS, you're invisible.

## What Is an ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System is software that:

1. **Parses** your resume into structured data fields
2. **Scores** it against the job description keywords
3. **Ranks** all applicants
4. Only forwards top-scoring resumes to recruiters

Common ATS platforms include Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, iCIMS, and Lever.

## How ATS Scores Your Resume

ATS systems evaluate resumes across several dimensions:

### 1. Keyword Match Rate
The single most important factor. ATS compares every word in your resume against the job description. If the job says "Python developer" and your resume says "Python programmer" — that's a mismatch.

**Fix:** Mirror the exact language from job postings.

### 2. Section Header Recognition
ATS looks for specific section headers:
- EXPERIENCE (not "Work History" or "Employment")
- EDUCATION (not "Academic Background")
- SKILLS (not "Competencies" or "Expertise")
- SUMMARY (not "Profile" or "About Me")

**Fix:** Use standard, exact section header names.

### 3. Text Parseability
ATS cannot read:
- Text inside images
- Text in tables or columns
- Text in headers/footers
- Fancy fonts that don't embed correctly

**Fix:** Use a simple, single-column layout with standard fonts.

### 4. Quantified Achievements
Modern ATS uses NLP to identify impact. Resumes with numbers score significantly higher:
- "Increased revenue by 40%" > "Helped with revenue growth"
- "Led team of 8 engineers" > "Managed a team"

**Fix:** Add metrics to every bullet point.

## The 6 Most Common ATS Mistakes

```
❌ Using tables for layout
❌ Including a photo or logo
❌ Using headers/footers for contact info
❌ Fancy bullet symbols (➤ ▸ ●)
❌ Multiple columns
❌ Missing required sections
```
